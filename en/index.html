Max Sepúlveda

Visual artist, artisan, and cultural manager.

Pottery

Creation of utilitarian and decorative pieces using ancestral techniques and designs.

Ceramics

Mold casting, modeling, sgraffito, engobes, and glazes. Exploration of traditional forms with a contemporary vision.

Embroidery

Exploration of 15 traditional stitches.

Batik & Embroidery

Contemporary fusion of batik and embroidery celebrating natural and cultural heritage.

Weaving & Textiles

Mapuche loom, rigid heddle loom, frame loom, artistic tapestry, Andean cordmaking, and textile jewelry.

Lanigraphy (Yarn Painting)

Technique of adhering wool yarns and cords onto wood or cardboard surfaces.

Resist Dyeing Techniques

Batik, silk painting, and tie-dye (shibori). Pre-Columbian and contemporary designs.

Audiovisual Production

Art direction for short films and documentaries documenting community creative processes.

Collaborative Art

Memory map embroideries, mixed embroidery and batik murals, painted murals, street parades, and artistic interventions in the natural and cultural landscape. Collaborative art as a vehicle for community participation and social transformation.
