Artist Residency in New Zealand

In 2018, the artist completed an artistic residency at the Dunedin School of Art at Otago Polytechnic, New Zealand, which included the solo exhibition titled Textiles and Pottery of Tagua Tagua.

In the school's ceramic studio, he conducted an ancestral pottery workshop using the Tagua Tagua technique for students, faculty, and local artists. He also led cultural mediation sessions in both the gallery and the main campus Hub.

Additionally, he published a research article in Scope Contemporary Research Topics: Art and Design detailing his experience during the residency.
