Chile-New Zealand Cultural Exchange

Solo exhibition held at the School of Art in Dunedin, as the result of an artistic residency in New Zealand.

The show presented a selection of ceramic and textile works inspired by the cultural identity of Tagua Tagua and its dialogue with Māori tradition.
