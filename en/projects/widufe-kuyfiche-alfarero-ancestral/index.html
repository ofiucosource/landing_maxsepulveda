Widüfe Kuyfiche – Ancestral Potter

Research and rescue of indigenous pottery from central Chile. FONDART 2006 project.

March 1, 2006
ResearchCeramicsFondartHeritage

Clay Makers

Project funded by the National Fund for Cultural Development and the Arts (FONDART).

It consisted of research and rescue of indigenous pottery from central Chile, culminating in an exhibition at the Sala Samuel Román at the Casa de la Cultura in Rancagua.

Widüfe in Mapudungun means 'potter' or 'clay maker'.
