Rescuing Coastal Memory

Winning project of the Competitive Funds for Culture from the Ministry of Education and Culture of Uruguay.

It consisted of creating a collective textile map of the coast of Rocha, embroidered on-site by residents and visitors in five coastal towns.

During the process, people shared anecdotes about places that left marks on their lives, stories of love, adventures and misadventures.
