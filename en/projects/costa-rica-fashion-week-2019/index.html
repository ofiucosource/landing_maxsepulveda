Project

Costa Rica Fashion Week 2019

Invitation to exhibit his work and pieces by the Tagua Tagua Textile Cooperative at Costa Rica Fashion Week 2019, including a lecture, batik workshop, and national media interviews.

August 1, 2019
TextileFashionInternationalCosta Rica

Textiles, Craft, and Contemporary Design

The artist was invited to Costa Rica Fashion Week 2019 to showcase his artworks alongside creations by the Tagua Tagua Textile Cooperative.

During his participation, he gave a lecture titled Social Intervention through Textile Art, taught a batik workshop, and was interviewed across national broadcast media.
